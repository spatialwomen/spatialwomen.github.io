SpatialW Webinar Series Launch: Building a Career in Academia

📅 Date: Friday, September 26, 2025
⏰ Time: 3:00–4:00 PM CDT / 4:00–5:00 PM EDT / 2:00–3:00 PM MDT

We are excited to launch the SpatialW Webinar Series with our first session focusing on academic career development. This interactive webinar features a panel discussion with distinguished faculty members:

  • Prof. Christine Bassem from Wellesley College
  • Prof. Jieun Lee from University of Northern Colorado

Why join? This session offers real stories and career guidance for Ph.D. students and early-career researchers interested in academia. The informal panel discussion includes live Q&A, giving you the chance to ask questions directly to our panelists!

Nov. 2024

DEI event @ ACM SIGSPATIAL '24

We co-organized the DEI event at ACM SIGSPATIAL '24 with the DEI conference co-chairs, involving 44 participants, including students, 3 organizers, 10 mentors assigned in advance, and researchers from academia, industry, and national labs. We hosted a lunchtime event on Day 1, where mentors shared their experiences with junior researchers and answered their questions.

Nov. 2023

Networking session for underrepresented groups @ ACM SIGSPATIAL '23

We organized the second networking event at the ACM SIGSPATIAL '23 with 17 participants and three invited mentors, Prof. Li Xiong, Dr. Lexie Yang, and Prof. Christine Bassem. We learned valuable networking strategies and heard about mentors' career development experiences and their Ph.D. lives.

Apr. 2023

Networking session for underrepresented groups @ SIAM SDM '23

We organized a unique networking event aimed at promoting diversity in computer science. Dr. Diane Oyen from Los Alamos National Laboratory gave an invited talk and shared her insights on career development. We had the opportunity to connect with researchers from diverse backgrounds and build meaningful professional relationships while supporting diversity in the field.

Invited Talk @ Thomas Jefferson Science and Technology High School

Nov. 2022

Women @ ACM SIGSPATIAL '22

We had the first networking event for female graduate students at ACM SIGSPATIAL '22, gathering at the public lounge at the Seattle Marriott Waterfront hotel. We shared our experiences as female researchers from various computer science and geospatial communities.
